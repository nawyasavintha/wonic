About

From hand-embroidered lehengas and the ivory Kandyan drape to a black evening gown under coloured studio light.

Kaweesha Randahara is a 22-year-old model with a long 5′8″ frame and light skin with cool undertones. She works mainly in bridal and traditional wear, and in studio fashion portraiture.

She holds a pose without looking stiff and can carry heavy jewellery and layered textiles. She has worked with several photographers and is open to bridal campaigns, fashion editorials, commercial print and runway.
